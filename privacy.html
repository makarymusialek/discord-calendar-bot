Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 October 2026

Events Google Calendar Bot is a Discord application designed to publish information about Discord Scheduled Events and provide convenient links that allow users to add those events to Google Calendar.

1. Information We Process

The bot processes only information necessary to provide its functionality. This may include:

  • Discord server (guild) IDs
  • Discord channel IDs
  • Discord Scheduled Event IDs
  • Discord message IDs created by the bot
  • Scheduled Event names
  • Scheduled Event descriptions
  • Scheduled Event start and end dates and times
  • Scheduled Event locations
  • Other information contained in Discord Scheduled Events that is required to display or synchronize them

2. Information We Do Not Collect

The bot does not:

  • read or store private Discord messages
  • collect general Discord message content
  • collect passwords or authentication credentials
  • access users' Google accounts
  • access users' private Google Calendars
  • use Google OAuth
  • sell personal data

3. Google Calendar Links

The bot generates standard Google Calendar URLs containing information from a Discord Scheduled Event. When a user clicks such a link, the user is redirected to Google Calendar.

The bot does not receive access to the user's Google account or calendar and cannot create, modify, synchronize, or delete events inside a user's private Google Calendar.

4. Data Storage

The bot may store limited configuration and technical data required for operation, including:

  • the Discord channel selected by a server administrator
  • mappings between Discord Scheduled Events and messages created by the bot
  • technical identifiers required to synchronize or update those messages

This information is used only to provide and maintain the bot's functionality.

5. Data Sharing

Data processed by the bot is not sold or rented to third parties. Information may be transmitted to Discord as part of normal bot operation and to Google only when a user voluntarily opens a generated Google Calendar link.

6. Data Retention and Removal

Server administrators may remove the bot from their Discord server at any time. Configuration data related to a server may also be removed when it is no longer required or upon a valid removal request.

7. Third-Party Services

The bot relies on Discord for Discord-related functionality and may generate links to Google Calendar. These third-party services operate under their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the bot's functionality or data-processing practices change. The current version will always be available on this page.

9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or data removal requests, please contact the developer through the application's official support server.
